# Community-assisted solo study

Question: does the community-track assisted agent wrapper — legal actions
enumerated in the observation, an explicit `ACTION:` final-line instruction,
and up to two invalid-reply retries with referee feedback — move the
separation-v2 models off their invalid-reply floors and up the score ladder?
The unassisted arm is the committed `studies/model-separation-v2` solo run;
this packet reruns the same models, the same pinned serve method, and the same
public seeds through `community-assisted:openai-chat/<model>:retries=2`.

## Answer

Assistance does not rescue the small models and does not change the study's
headline answer. Every model that scored below random unassisted still scores
below random assisted; the invalid-reply floor the wrapper targets survives
for both small models; only 7B — which was already nearly format-clean —
converts assistance into clean turns and a higher score. Ordering is still not
size-separated (minesweeper rho 0.5; 1.5B scores below 0.5B).

## Method

- Pinned serve: llama.cpp b11662 `llama-server`, `--ctx-size 4096 --temp 0.0
  --seed 42 --threads 2` (the v2 study's box had 8 threads; this box is
  2-core, so thread count was adapted — the only serve deviation), one
  warm-up call.
- Run: `community-assisted:openai-chat/<stem>:retries=2`, `--concurrency 1
  --max-output-tokens 256 --call-timeout-ms 60000 --max-calls 2500`,
  community track, suites generated by `make-suite.mjs` with the v2 public
  seeds (`gauntlet-public-assisted-v1-<model>-<tier>-<n>`).
- Tiers: `mastermind/mini` (the invalid-rate tier; random mean 0.0298) and
  `minesweeper/beginner` (the only score-discriminating tier; random mean
  0.6625), 8 public seeds per tier for 0.5B/1.5B, 4 for 7B — the same seeds
  the unassisted arm ran. The four zero-floor tiers are not rerun: the
  unassisted arm shows all models at 0 there, the assisted wrapper cannot
  fix planning failures, and assisted 0.5B sliding measured ~20 min/episode
  on this box; the cut is recorded rather than hidden.
- Scoring: the official scorer (`scoring/score.js`, replay-verifies every
  trajectory). `stats.json` is deterministic (seeded bootstraps).
- Provenance fix: `compute-stats.mjs`'s `pinned` header was corrected to
  record the community-track run shape and `--threads 2`.

## Results (rhae-v1; invalidReplyRate = share of turns whose reply stayed
unparseable after up to 2 retries; secPerCall is wall time per agent call)

### mastermind/mini — random mean 0.0298

| model | unassisted mean → assisted mean | invalidReply unassisted → assisted | secPerCall |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5B | 0 → 0 | 1.00 → 1.00 | 0.82 → 1.45 |
| 1.5B | 0 → 0 | 0.455 → **1.00** | 1.23 → 4.40 |
| 7B | 0.5 → 0.5 | 0.019 → 0 | 6.16 → 40.50 |

### minesweeper/beginner — random mean 0.6625

| model | unassisted mean → assisted mean | invalidReply unassisted → assisted | secPerCall |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5B | 0.116 → 0.354 | 0.676 → 0.522 | 0.98 → 7.04 |
| 1.5B | 0.192 → 0.195 | 0.818 → 0.692 | 1.25 → 34.03 |
| 7B | 0.380 → **0.563** | 0.636 → **0** | 6.11 → 54.51 |

Ordering on minesweeper: means 0.354 (0.5B) / 0.195 (1.5B) / 0.563 (7B) —
1.5B below 0.5B (paired diff −0.158, CI [−0.387, 0], spans zero), rho 0.5.
BeatRandom: all three still below the random interval; only 1.5B is
*separated* from random and it is separated downward, same as the unassisted
arm.

## Findings

1. **The floor is not a formatting problem assistance can fix.** The 1.5B
   seed-0 mastermind episode shows the mechanism: the model replies
   `guess: ABCD` / `ACTION: guess ABCD` — a syntactically perfect final line —
   but the guess is not one of the legal actions. The wrapper retried with
   referee feedback three times (`attempts: 3, exhausted: true`) and the
   episode still ended `forfeit-invalid` at the 3-strike threshold. The model
   ignores the enumerated legal actions, so retries just burn calls. Every
   0.5B and 1.5B mastermind episode ends this way.
2. **1.5B mastermind got worse under assistance** (invalidReply 0.455 → 1.00):
   unassisted it sometimes emitted a legal guess; with the augmented
   observation it never did. The longer observation is not helping this
   model parse the task. (No capping is involved: replies are short.)
3. **7B is the one clean win, and it was already nearly clean.** Assistance
   takes 7B from 0.019 invalid replies to 0 on mastermind (score unchanged at
   0.5 — above the 0.0298 random floor both arms) and from 0.636 to 0 on
   minesweeper, lifting the mean 0.380 → 0.563 (+0.183). It still does not
   separate from the random interval (0.6625) — above-random remains answered
   NO for every size on minesweeper, assisted or not.
4. **Cost is real and falls hardest on the capable model.** Prompt growth
   (legal actions + retry feedback) multiplies secPerCall roughly 5-9x across
   the board; 7B minesweeper calls average 54.5 s against 6.1 s unassisted on
   this box. Assistance buys clean turns, not efficiency.

## Deviations

- `--threads 2` instead of 8 (2-core box), recorded in `stats.json.pinned`.
- Zero-floor tiers cut for wall-clock budget; both measured tiers are the two
  the v2 study drew its conclusions from, and the seeds match exactly.
- `runs/estimate.test.js` and `cli-e2e` were not touched by the branch.

## Reproduce

`bash reproduce.sh` (requires the sandbox-local GGUFs and llama.cpp build;
~25 min wall on the 2-core box, dominated by 7B mastermind under memory
pressure — prompt eval dropped to ~14 tok/s with the 7B model resident).
